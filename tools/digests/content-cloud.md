Process
The design process for this project was divided into four key phases to ensure a user centered and results experience: The design process for this project was divide 1. Discovery Conducting competitor analysis, digital content trend research, and user interviews to gather insights and understand the key needs and challenges faced by content creators. 2. ## block b05.jpg [TEXT] h=387
Discovery
Discover and Understanding The first stage of the design process begins with gaining a deep understanding of the main problems faced by content creators and brands in today's digital age. Through industry trend analysis and case studies of popular content distribution platforms, 1 identified that many users feel overwhelmed by inefficient content production processes, a lack of insight into their audience, and confusion about when and what to post. These insights form the foundation for developing a design direction that is not only aesthetically pleasing but also strategically relevant and beneficial.
Competitor Analysis
To understand Futuri Content Cloud's position in the market, I conducted a competitor analysis of several similar platforms that have similar functions, which is to support content strategies based on data and Al. This analysis covers key features, strengths, weaknesses, and the design approaches they employ. From this case, I found that many platforms excel in one area but lack integration in combining audience insights, content strategies, and collaboration tools. This finding presents an opportunity for Futuri to position itself as a more cohesive end to end solution.

Pain Points From analyzing various content and social media platforms, we found some common pain points that users experience: 1. Many platforms only focus on scheduling, without supporting the ideation process or long term content strategy. 2. The interface is complicated and confusing for beginners, making it hard to onboard new users. 3. Insights and analytics are shallow, not helpful enough for strategy based decision making. 4. High subscription costs, especially on platforms with comprehensive features, making access difficult for independent creators or small businesses. 5. Lack of seamless integration between content planning, audience analytics, and team collaboration processes. 6. Many platforms feel too corporate or rigid, with user interfaces and experiences that are less engaging for the creative industry.
User & Audience

Social media managers who require efficiency in scheduling and reporting content performance. 3. Small business owners who produce their own content and want audience insights without needing a large team. 4. Marketing teams at agencies or startups that require a collaborative platform with editorial control, insights, and flexible 5. Communication teams at large companies that need in depth analytics solutions, approval systems, and multi platform integration, automation

Usability Testing To evaluate the overall effectiveness, usability, and user satisfaction of the Futuri Content Cloud platform, I conducted a series of moderated usability testing sessions with participants representing potential users of the platform. These sessions were carefully structured to simulate real world usage, using realistic scenarios combined with goal oriented tasks that reflect a new user's journey when discovering and exploring Futuri Content Cloud. The main goal of this usability testing was to observe how users interact with key features, how easily they can navigate the interface, and whether the design choices, such as content hierarchy, button placement, and terminology successfully guide users toward achieving their goals. Furthermore, I aimed to identify any usability issues or pain points that could hinder the overall user experience and determine whether the platform effectively aligns with user needs, expectations, and motivations. By analyzing participant behavior, feedback, and performance in completing tasks, I was able to gather valuable insights to inform potential design improvements. These findings also helped validate whether the current product direction supports users through every stage of their content creation journey from discovery and exploration to engagement and confidence. Participants were given the following scenario to complete: 1. You are interested in becoming a content creator but are still unsure of what to do, and you have heard that Futuri Content Cloud can solve your problems. Now you want to know more about Futuri Content Cloud, so what do you do? 2. You see that Futuri Content Cloud provides a community for sharing information related to content creators, and you want to join. What can you do? 3. You understand Futuri Content Cloud, but you still want to explore its features. How do you do that? 4. Right now, you're facing challenges and feeling confused. What should you do? 5. You also want to know the latest news about Futuri Content Cloud to build your confidence in using this platform. What should you do? 6. You are 100% confident in using Futuri Content Cloud. What can you do?
