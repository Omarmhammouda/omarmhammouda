Overview

NuVIE helps people keep up with what they watch across streaming services: track shows and movies, manage favorites, and get recommendations that actually fit. The warm orange system keeps a utility app feeling personal.

The case study runs from authentication and onboarding through the recommendation feature and watchlist management, closing with usability testing and the fixes it drove.
