Overview

Karma is a community platform concept built around one promise: help groups start, grow, and keep healthy online spaces. The visual system pairs deep greens with generous whitespace, so dense community activity still reads calmly.

The case study walks the full arc, from research into how existing community tools handle moderation and onboarding, through user flows and wireframes, to a complete high-fidelity UI.
