Overview

HM is an e-commerce back office: the screens a store team lives in to track digital purchases, product reviews, and customer activity. The hard problems are density and orientation, so the case study leans on breadcrumb navigation, consistent tables, and a sidebar that always tells you where you are.

Every management view is annotated with the reasoning behind it, from how the purchase tables paginate to why the activity log sits inside the purchase detail page.

Inside the case study

  • Information architecture
  • Digital purchase management
  • Product and review tables
  • Purchase detail and activity log
  • Sidebar navigation system
