Overview

Duradry sells a considered solution to an uncomfortable problem, so the design leads with confidence and clarity: bold product photography, plain-language explanations of how the product works, and a path to purchase with no detours.

The case study covers the product pages, the how-it-works content, bundles and offers, and the supporting FAQ, each annotated with the reasoning behind the layout.
