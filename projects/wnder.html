W*nder

An educational CBD brand site, from sitemap to storefront.

Type
Brand site
Platform
Responsive web
Deliverables

Overview

W*nder sells a product many buyers still have questions about, so the site teaches before it sells. Plain-language education sits next to product storytelling, walking a first-time buyer from curiosity to a confident purchase.

The case study covers the sitemap, style exploration, and the page designs themselves, in a deep navy system with warm accents that keeps the brand feeling considered rather than clinical.
