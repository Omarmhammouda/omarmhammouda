Overview

Oh Snap pairs a consumer app with the system that powers it. The app side covers onboarding, discovery, and messaging; the system side is a complete UI kit, from color and type scales down to keyboards and component states.

The case study shows both layers side by side, which is exactly how the work happened: every screen built from the kit, and the kit refined by what the screens needed.
