How I lead

Lead work is mostly influence: getting design into the room early, and making the reasoning easy for everyone to follow.

  1. Design in the room earlyProcess

    At Fiserv I pushed for design to join product and technical discussions before requirements were locked, not after. It led to more collaborative design reviews, clearer handoffs, and usability problems caught before they were built.

  2. Push back with optionsStakeholders

    When requirements made a flow harder for the people using it, I challenged them, always bringing alternatives that still worked for the business and for engineering.

  3. Systems over screensCraft

    I help teams think past the screen in front of them: shared patterns, components and accessibility that hold up across a whole product.

  4. Explain the whyTeaching

    In critiques, reviews and stakeholder walkthroughs, I focus on the reasoning behind a decision, not just the pixels. Teaching is the part of design I love most, and I'm looking for a role where growing other designers is part of the job.

About

Research first, pixels second. I design for the people who have to use the product every day, not just the ones who buy it.
