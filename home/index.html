About

Research first, pixels second. I design for the people who have to use the product every day, not just the ones who buy it.

I've spent eight years in product design across fintech, SaaS, CRM and e-commerce, most recently leading UX at Verisk and before that at Fiserv. I'm happiest inside messy, rule-heavy systems, finding the structure that makes them feel simple.

Outside of product work I'm a photographer (the bridge at the top is my shot), and I love teaching design. I'm open to senior and lead roles, remote or hybrid in New Jersey and New York.
